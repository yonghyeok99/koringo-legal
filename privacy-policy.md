# KORINGO Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 27, 2026
**Operator:** Made In Hanguk (Yonghyeok Kim, publisher registered in the Republic of Korea) ("we", "us")
**Contact:** kwh5723@gmail.com

KORINGO is a Korean-learning app for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and who else may process it. The short version: **KORINGO has no accounts and no ads. Your learning progress stays on your device.** Three things can leave your device, and each is explained below: anonymous usage statistics (which you can turn off), an email address if you choose to give us one, and purchase receipts when you buy Premium.

## 1. Information stored on your device only

The app keeps the following on your device so it can work. None of it is sent to us. We cannot see it.

- Learning progress: completed lessons, level-check result, quiz results, streak days, review schedule, wrong answers you are revisiting.
- Gogumas (in-app hearts) and their refill timer.
- Saved words, unlocked word packs, and words you practiced writing.
- Settings: sound, voice speed, reduce motion, reminder on/off, and whether anonymous usage data is shared.
- Achievements you have unlocked and whether you have been asked to rate the app.

You can erase your learning data at any time from **Profile → Reset all progress**, or by uninstalling the app. Purchases and settings are not affected by a reset (see section 4).

## 2. What we do not do

- We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- We do not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, or microphone audio.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not sell personal information.
- Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. There is no push server and no notification data leaves your device.
- Your quiz answers and the Korean you type or write are never sent anywhere.

## 3. Anonymous usage statistics (Amplitude)

To learn which lessons people finish and where they stop, the app sends a small set of usage events to **Amplitude** (Amplitude, Inc.), an analytics service. The data is stored on Amplitude's servers in the **European Union**.

What is sent:

- a random device identifier generated by the app (not your name, email, or advertising ID),
- app usage events: a lesson or course completed, the level check finished, the Premium screen shown, running out of gogumas, the result of a purchase attempt, and whether you chose to give an email address (the address itself is never sent to Amplitude),
- the course and lesson identifiers involved, plus app version, operating system, device model, language, and the country derived from your IP address by Amplitude (the IP address itself is not stored by us).

What is not sent: screen recordings, your answers, the words you save or write, and any free text.

**You can turn this off** at any time in **Profile → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data**. When it is off, no events are sent. Amplitude's privacy policy: https://amplitude.com/privacy

## 4. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store or Google Play and are subject to their terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment card or billing details.

To recognise your subscription across app launches and devices, the app uses **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

- an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID (not linked to your name or email),
- the purchase receipt or token issued by the store,
- basic device information such as platform and app version, and
- the same random device identifier used for Amplitude (section 3), so that subscription events (trial started, renewed, cancelled) can be counted in our usage statistics without identifying you.

RevenueCat uses this only to validate purchases and report subscription status back to the app. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

If you want a refund or to cancel, use the store's subscription settings on your device; we cannot process refunds directly.

## 5. Email (optional)

After you finish the Hangul course, and in a few other places, the app may offer to send you a free Hangul cheat sheet (PDF) by email. **Giving an email address is optional.** You can skip it, and the app does not ask again after the first time.

If you enter an address, the app sends it to our mailing-list provider, **Kit** (Kit, Inc., formerly ConvertKit), together with where in the app you signed up, the app version, and your device language. We use it to send the PDF and, occasionally, Korean learning tips and news about KORINGO. You will first receive a confirmation email and are only added to the list after you confirm (double opt-in).

Every email has an unsubscribe link in the footer. Unsubscribing removes your address from the list. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to kwh5723@gmail.com. Your email address is never shared with Amplitude or RevenueCat and is not sold.

Kit processes your address on our behalf and stores it on its servers in the United States. Kit's privacy policy: https://kit.com/privacy

## 6. Handwriting recognition (Writing practice)

The writing feature can recognise what you wrote by hand. Recognition runs **on your device** using Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you use it, the app downloads a Korean recognition model from Google's servers; that download is an ordinary network request and Google may receive standard connection information such as your IP address. Your handwriting strokes are processed locally and are not uploaded. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 7. Speech

Korean audio in the app is bundled with the app. If a sentence has no bundled recording, the app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine, which is governed by your device manufacturer's or operating system's privacy policy.

## 8. Children

KORINGO is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). The app has no accounts, and the email offer is optional. If you believe a child has given us an email address, write to us and we will delete it. A parent can remove all app data by uninstalling the app.

## 9. Security

Learning data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption, backups). If you back up your device to iCloud or Google, your app data may be included in that backup under the store's own policies. Data sent to Amplitude, RevenueCat, and Kit travels over encrypted connections.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, California, or Korea), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal information.

- **Learning data:** it is only on your device; use Reset all progress or uninstall.
- **Usage statistics:** turn off sharing in Profile → Privacy. The events already sent are tied only to a random device identifier; write to us with that in mind and we will help where we can.
- **Email address:** use the unsubscribe link, or write to us to have it deleted.
- **Purchase records:** contact Apple, Google, or RevenueCat as described in section 4.

You can always contact us at the address above.

## 11. Changes

If the app starts handling information differently, we will update this policy, change the effective date, and note the change in the app or store listing.
