# KORINGO Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 24, 2026
**Operator:** Made In Hanguk (Yonghyeok Kim, publisher registered in the Republic of Korea) ("we", "us")
**Contact:** kwh5723@gmail.com

KORINGO is a Korean-learning app for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and who else may process it. The short version: **KORINGO has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your learning data stays on your device.** The only outside parties involved are the app stores and their payment processing partner when you buy a subscription, and Google when the handwriting model is downloaded.

## 1. Information stored on your device only

The app keeps the following on your device so it can work. None of it is sent to us. We cannot see it.

- Learning progress: completed lessons, level-check result, quiz results, streak days, review schedule, wrong answers you are revisiting.
- Gogumas (in-app hearts) and their refill timer.
- Saved words, unlocked word packs, and words you practiced writing.
- Settings: sound, voice speed, reduce motion, reminder on/off.
- Achievements you have unlocked and whether you have been asked to rate the app.

You can erase all of this at any time from **Profile → Reset all progress**, or by uninstalling the app. Purchases are not affected by a reset (see section 3).

## 2. What we do not do

- We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or microphone audio.
- We do not use advertising SDKs, advertising identifiers, or analytics/tracking services.
- We do not sell or share personal information.
- Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. There is no push server and no notification data leaves your device.

## 3. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store or Google Play and are subject to their terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment card or billing details.

To recognise your subscription across app launches and devices, the app uses **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

- an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID (not linked to your name or email),
- the purchase receipt or token issued by the store, and
- basic device information such as platform and app version.

RevenueCat uses this only to validate purchases and report subscription status back to the app. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

If you want a refund or to cancel, use the store's subscription settings on your device; we cannot process refunds directly.

## 4. Handwriting recognition (Writing practice)

The writing feature can recognise what you wrote by hand. Recognition runs **on your device** using Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you use it, the app downloads a Korean recognition model from Google's servers; that download is an ordinary network request and Google may receive standard connection information such as your IP address. Your handwriting strokes are processed locally and are not uploaded. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Speech

Korean audio in the app is bundled with the app. If a sentence has no bundled recording, the app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine, which is governed by your device manufacturer's or operating system's privacy policy.

## 6. Children

KORINGO is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). Because the app has no accounts and collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on request; a parent can remove all app data by uninstalling the app.

## 7. Security

Data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption, backups). If you back up your device to iCloud or Google, your app data may be included in that backup under the store's own policies.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, California, or Korea), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information. Since we hold no personal information about you, these rights are satisfied by the on-device controls described in section 1. For purchase records, contact Apple, Google, or RevenueCat as described in section 3. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help where we can.

## 9. Changes

If the app starts handling information differently (for example, if we add accounts or cloud sync), we will update this policy, change the effective date, and note the change in the app or store listing.
